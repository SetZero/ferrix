# Handover, 2026-10-03: the channel round trip toward seL4

One coordinating session ran the plan of `docs/OPAQUE-KERNEL.md` §9.5 from
the evening of 2026-10-02 to the wind-down on 2026-10-03, with two
engineering subagents (sessions A and B), measurement subagents and a
certification-consultant subagent of its own. This page says what was asked,
what was decided and measured, what is on `main`, what is on branches, and
what the next session does first. Local paths are on nazuna.

## What the customer asked, and decided

- **Continue §9.5**, then **retarget it**: Ferrix's round trip as good as
  seL4's or better, not 1.5 times it (§9.6). The gate figure is the matched
  one: every mitigation on, both programs in one speculation domain, against
  seL4 with the same protections. `--mitigations off` is reported, not gated.
- **§9.5's four decisions:** the matched figure; the vector-state contract
  for native calls that block (yes; native ABI only); a fast path for 0x1013
  (yes, subject to the consultant); the consultant's seat taken by a subagent
  of the session running the plan.
- **§9.7's three:** `ferrix.fastpath` off in the certified configuration for
  the first release (on in development and the perf rows); step 4b built,
  after step 5, under its own design review; the residual risk of a tested
  equivalence, not a proof, accepted.
- **§9.8's Q10:** FDP_RIP.2 widened to "the register state a program is
  given at every switch" (it covers 3a's reset).
- **The race model:** `loom` in its own workspace, `src/tests/loom/`, like
  `src/tests/fuzz`; never in the root workspace, whose `deny.toml` bans
  `libc`.
- **Block I/O faster than Redox:** a BACKLOG row.
- **Pushing stays the customer's.** The session landed on `main` under
  `land.sh` and never pushed.

## How the work went, for the next coordinator

- **Take `land.sh`'s lock first, then compare `main`.** `main` moved every
  twenty minutes or so; a gate on a base that has moved is not the landing's
  gate. The fleet rule held: rebase under the lock, and where the new
  commits meet only derived files, `check` plus one boot, otherwise re-gate.
- **One piece, one review.** Every piece came back "OK IF", and meeting its
  conditions took one to two hours. The consultant found real defects every
  time: a lost close wake, `NoMemory` ending the rings, F-60, 3b's write-skip,
  the 65-signal cap.
- **A VS Code restart leaves the old instance running.** It kept its agents
  and went on landing under the same session id. Check `~/.claude/sessions/`
  for a second process with the same `sessionId` before acting.
- **Controls that cannot fire** (a window no boot reaches) were replaced
  by ones that can, or by a `loom` model, each time with the consultant's
  agreement.
